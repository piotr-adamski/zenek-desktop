## Project artwork

The bundled ZENN character was created by project owner Piotr Adamski as a custom
pet. The original spritesheet, copied animation frames and the locally generated
fallback Zenek artwork are included under the repository's Apache-2.0 license.
Frame hashes and timing are in the asset manifests. No stock Codex/OpenAI pet
